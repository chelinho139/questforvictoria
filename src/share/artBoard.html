Quest For Victoria

Help pick the art style

Quest For Victoria is a co-op adventure game with an isometric view. Every card draws the same little forest: a hero, a cow, a slime, some trees and a path. We have settled on the Silhouette style. Below is the shortlist of eight looks, followed by six new variants built from them. The full round and earlier rounds are further down.

  1. Mark your favourites with the Favourite button. Pick as many as you like.
  2. Leave a note on any card: what works, what doesn’t, what you would change.
  3. Copy your feedback with the button at the bottom and paste it into your reply.
